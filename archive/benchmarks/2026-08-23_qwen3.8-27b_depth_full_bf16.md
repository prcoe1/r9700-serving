## Takeaways

- **Depth-0 prefill is markedly slower than the 08-22 baseline** (pp2048
  ~1989 vs ~2628 t/s) and the deep-prefill rows are uniformly ~5–20% down
  (pp256K 876 vs 953, pp200K 1019 vs 1120, pp64K 1631 vs 1909). The deep-prefill
  deficit versus 08-22 is the same direction as the 08-22 note's bf16-vs-fp8
  finding but on a per-build basis it reads more like build-to-build variance /
  GPU state than a KV-dtype effect (both runs here and in 08-22 are bf16).
- **Decode is flatter**: tg32 sits in the ~40–62 t/s band across all depths,
  with no consistent monotonic trend; the 08-22 rows were similar.
- **TTFT scales ~linearly with depth** (1.03 s @0 → 2.9 @4K → 294 s @256K),
  attention-bound as expected for bf16 KV at the deep end.
- Coherence passed at every depth (no NaN, no MTP token loops).
