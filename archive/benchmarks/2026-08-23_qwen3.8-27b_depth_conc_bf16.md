# 2026-08-23 Qwen3.8-27B — depth + concurrency sweep (bf16 KV default)

Default-profile sweep on the stock build (vLLM v0.28.0rc2, torch 2.13, AITER
v0.1.20, `ROCM_AITER_UNIFIED_ATTN`, MTP3, TP=2, 256K max-model-len,
`--gpu-memory-utilization 0.95`, `--max-num-seqs 2`), bf16 KV
(`VLLM_KV_CACHE_DTYPE=bfloat16`, the current default), calibrated fp8 sidecar
on disk but not active. Both sweeps used llama-benchy 0.4.0, `--runs 3`,
`--enable-prefix-caching`, thinking off. Coherence **PASSED** both sweeps.

## Depth sweep (pp2048/tg128, concurrency=1)

Depth is previous-conversation tokens; `ctx_pp`/`ctx_tg` load the deep prefix,
then `pp2048`/`tg128` are the new-token prefill/generation on top.

|------:|-----------:|--------------:|-----------:|----------:|
| 0     |            |            — |    1962.20 |     61.71 |
| 4096  |    2112.62 |        1941.46 |     698.67 |     59.91 |
| 16384 |    2068.56 |        7923.40 |     365.39 |     62.73 |
| 32768 |    1915.61 |       17109.45 |     665.46 |     61.49 |

Note: with prefix caching enabled, `pp2048` re-processes only the 2048 new
tokens on top of the (cached) prefix, so its t/s reflects the new-token
prefill cost, not full depth+2048. The `ctx_*` rows show the deep-prefix load
cost (TTFT scales ~linearly with depth: ~1.9 s @4K → ~17.1 s @32K).

## Takeaways

- **Best aggregate throughput is at concurrency 2**: ~2105 t/s prefill and
  ~114 t/s decode (total), versus ~1962 / ~66 at c1. TP=2 only saturates with
  ~2 in flight.
- **Beyond c2 it degrades sharply.** `--max-num-seqs 2` gates how many requests
  are actually resident; c4/c8 back up into the queue, TTFT balloons
  (3.8 s → 7.8 s) and per-request throughput drops (707 → 516 pp, 52 → 47 tg)
  while total pp falls (1314 → 1127) — the queue adds latency without adding
  throughput.
- **The `max-num-seqs 2` cap** (from `vllm#35288` MTP concurrency corruption
  mitigation) is the binding constraint on this stack: it is what makes c4/c8
  queue instead of co-scheduling. Raising it would require the MTP fix
  (`#35288`) to land first.
- **Depth affects new-token prefill rate non-monotonically** (698 @4K → 365
  @16K → 665 @32K), likely prefix-cache block geometry / align-mode effects on
  the hybrid; decode stays flat (~59–63 t/s) regardless of depth.
