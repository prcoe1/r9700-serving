## Runtime incident — protocol.py overlay (no rebuild needed)

First boot crash-looped: `ModuleNotFoundError:
vllm.entrypoints.chat_utils` at `import vllm`. Root cause: the
`compose.yaml` read-only mount of `patches/vllm/protocol.py` was locked to
v0.31.0, which still had `vllm/entrypoints/chat_utils.py`; rc0 removed that
module (names moved to `generate/base/protocol.py`). Fix: rebuilt the
overlay from rc0's `protocol.py` + the identical `tools: []` tolerance hunk
(diff vs rc0 shows exactly the 2 intended hunks), refreshed the stale
version note in `compose.yaml` (it still said v0.30.0). Lesson for future
bumps: the overlay is a second version-locked artifact outside
`patches/*.patch` — re-verify its base imports on every `VLLM_REF` change
before `up`.
