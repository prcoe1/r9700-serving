# 2026-10-06 — gfx1201 UA re-tune under ROCm 10.1 (keys hold, no change)

Re-ran `tools/tune_ua_config.py` in the rebuilt `vllm-0.31.0+rocm101.gfx1201`
image (server down, clean timings) to check the carried
`patches/aiter/unified-attention-gfx1201-tune.patch` keys still win under
the 10.1 compiler/Triton codegen and against upstream's newly merged
gfx1201 UA-2D D=256 tune (#5790, in the pinned post1). Raw sweep:
`~/.vllm-workspace/ua_sweep_2026-10-06.json`. Geometry: Qwen3.8 full-attn
slice (GQA 24:4, D=256, bf16 KV); scenarios d512–d256k decode, p2k/p2k_c64k
prefill. Baseline in every scenario is the live (patched) JSON.

## Result: hold — no patch change, no rebuild

- **3D decode** (our `D_LEQ_256` w4/s1/eu6 key): best challenger in every
  scenario is the *identical* triple `3d:w4:s1:eu6` at 1.01–1.05x on stable
  long-context runs (d64k–d256k, q4 variants) — run-order bias (candidates
  time after the baseline with warmer caches/clocks), not a real delta.
  d1k reads 1.33x on a 0.1 ms timing (29 µs absolute; same-identical-config
  noise, amplified). Nothing with different knobs beats it; eu8/s2 trail
  at 0.5–0.8x, s2 uniformly `invalid (lds-overflow)` — the LDS-guard
  rationale is unchanged under 10.1 codegen.
- **2D prefill** (our `Q_GEQ_256` w8 key): best challengers tie at
  0.985–0.991x (p2k, p2k_c64k). Upstream's #5790 D=256 keys do not beat it.
- **Correctness**: `max_diff = 0.0` on every valid candidate (bitwise
  identical modulo reduction order). No new overflow class: the narrowed
  `Q_LEQ_1` TILE-32 guard + upstream #4868 bounds stand.

## Reading identical-config "wins"

The sweep overrides only the `attn` triple (`num_warps`, `num_stages`,
`waves_per_eu`); reduce/kv_split stay as loaded. When the winning label
matches the baseline triple exactly (as in all 3D scenarios here), the vs-base
ratio measures sweep noise + warm-up bias, and anything ≤ ~1.05x on stable
timings (or large on sub-ms timings) is a tie. A real challenger must carry
*different* knobs *and* a stable-margin win — none appeared.
