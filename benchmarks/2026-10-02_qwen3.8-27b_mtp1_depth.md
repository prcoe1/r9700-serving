## Finding

MTP3 holds its ~1.3x decode edge all the way to 65K, then falls off a
cliff: at ~110K it is at MTP1 parity (35.3 vs 34.9; second MTP3 sweep 40.4
confirms the collapse, not noise). That is the #47602 acceptance-decay
shape manifesting live — draft acceptance decays with context until the
spec win evaporates. MTP1 degrades smoothly (attention-cost scaling only)
and strictly dominates MTP-off (~35% faster at every depth).

Decision: **keep MTP1** — best perf at long context (≥65K, ties-or-better
at ~110K with cheaper prefill). If the workload ever concentrates at
≤65K shallow/short, MTP3 is still ~1.3x on decode; the crossover sits
between 65K and 110K (a finer ladder — 80K/96K rungs — would pin it).

Prefix probe on MTP1: 0% incremental / coherence PASS, geometry unchanged
(block 1600, align). #55766 masking analysis unchanged (trigger chunk now
1+1=2 tokens; bad checkpoint still unrestorable under the 1-block
back-off).
