# 2026-10-07 — chunk-size A/B under live MTP1: 1024 vs 2048

**TL;DR**: kept 1024. At 3 victims + 106k co-prefill (cap 4, MTP1 K=1, fp8 KV,
v0.31.0 final), 2048 buys −17% big-prefill TTFT (54.2→44.9s) but costs ~1.7x
co-decode ITL during the prefill (p50 368→635ms, p99 688→896ms). Same
tradeoff as the 09-21 spec-off A/B, sharper under MTP1. Smoothness wins for
interactive multi-session work.

## Setup

Live profile, only `VLLM_MAX_BATCHED_TOKENS` changed (restart between arms).
Method: `benchmarks/conc_itl_probe.py qwen3.8-27b 110000 <label>` — 3 victims
(~16k prompt, 512-token streams, slots 4/4) + B (~106k prompt, fired after 32
batches). Matches the 10-06 c4-hold geometry. tokens/batch ~1.8 both arms
(MTP1 acceptance healthy throughout).

## Results

| metric | 1024 (kept) | 2048 |
|:-------|----:|----:|
| B TTFT (~106k prompt) | 54.2 s | 44.9 s (−17%) |
| victim ITL during B-prefill p50 | ~368 ms | ~635 ms |
| victim ITL during B-prefill p99 | ~688 ms | ~896 ms |
| post-B decode p50 | ~44 ms | ~44 ms (identical) |
| max_waiting | 1 | 2 |

Note on the probe's pooled choke ratio: 2.0x (1024) vs 20.3x (2048) is NOT
comparable across arms — at 1024 the pre-B window still overlapped the
victims' own 16k-prefill contention (pre-B p50 ~345ms vs ~44ms at 2048), which
inflates the 1024 denominator. The raw B-prefill windows above are the
apples-to-apples comparison.
